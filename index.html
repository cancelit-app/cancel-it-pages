Your Gmail connection stays under your control.

Gmail access is optional and separate from your Menvori account login. Menvori uses read-only Gmail access for user-initiated subscription scans.

  • Menvori does not send, edit, or delete your Gmail messages.
  • Google OAuth credentials are handled server-side.
  • You can disconnect Gmail from Menvori.
  • Menvori does not automatically cancel subscriptions for you.
