Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 5, 2026

Cancel It helps users identify and manage recurring subscriptions. This Privacy Policy explains what information we access, how we use it, what we store, and the choices available to users.

Account Information

When you create a Cancel It account, we collect your email address for authentication and account management. Authentication is provided using Supabase.

Google and Gmail Data

Connecting Gmail is optional and is separate from signing in to Cancel It.

If you choose to connect Gmail, Cancel It requests read-only Gmail access. Gmail is scanned only when you manually start a scan. We use this access solely to identify emails that may relate to recurring subscriptions, billing, renewals, and cancellation information.

Cancel It does not send, modify, delete, archive, or otherwise alter your Gmail messages.

What Gmail Data Is Stored

Raw Gmail message bodies, email subjects, senders, attachments, and Gmail message IDs are not stored in our application database.

Cancel It may store derived subscription information identified from your emails, such as:

  • Merchant or service name
  • Subscription amount
  • Currency
  • Billing frequency
  • Estimated or detected renewal information

You may manually edit or correct this derived subscription information inside the app.

Google OAuth Tokens

Google OAuth credentials used to maintain your Gmail connection are stored securely on the server and are not stored on your device as application data.

How We Use Google User Data

Google user data is used only to provide the user-facing subscription detection and management features described in this policy.

Cancel It's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Cancellation Links

Cancel It may help you locate an official cancellation page for a detected subscription. Cancellation pages are opened in your device's system browser.

Cancel It does not automatically cancel subscriptions or make purchases on your behalf.

Sharing and Selling of Data

We do not sell your personal information or Gmail data. We do not use Gmail data for advertising.

The current version of Cancel It does not include third-party advertising, behavioral tracking, or third-party analytics SDKs.

Gmail Disconnection

You may disconnect Gmail at any time from the Settings screen. Disconnecting Gmail removes the stored Gmail connection credentials used by Cancel It. Existing subscription records previously derived from your scans may remain in your Cancel It account until you remove your account.

Account Deletion

You can permanently delete your Cancel It account from the Settings screen in the app. Account deletion removes your Cancel It account, stored subscription information, Gmail connection information, and associated authentication data.

Data Security

We use technical and organizational safeguards designed to protect account and application data. Gmail authorization credentials are handled server-side and are not exposed to the mobile application.

Children's Privacy

Cancel It is not specifically designed for children.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy as the application changes. The latest version will always be published on this page with an updated revision date.

Contact

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or your data, contact us at: cancelit.support@gmail.com
