Gmail Connection

Cancel It uses read-only Gmail access to help identify recurring subscriptions. Gmail scanning occurs only when you start a scan.

If your Gmail connection expires or stops working, open Settings and select Reconnect Gmail.

You can disconnect Gmail at any time from Settings.

Subscription Detection

Subscription information detected by Cancel It may not always be perfect. You can manually correct detected merchant names, amounts, currencies, and billing frequencies inside the app.

Cancelling a Subscription

Cancel It can help locate cancellation information and may open an official cancellation page in your device's browser.

Cancel It does not automatically cancel subscriptions on your behalf.

Password Help

If you forget your password, use the password reset option on the Cancel It login screen.

Delete Your Account

You can permanently delete your Cancel It account directly inside the app:

Settings → Delete my account

Account deletion permanently removes your Cancel It account and associated application data.
